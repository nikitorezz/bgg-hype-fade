Does the hype fade?

How board-game ratings change after launch: 6,927 new releases on BoardGameGeek, each followed for three years, from ten years of daily ranking snapshots.

Launch ratings oversell. The typical new game on BoardGameGeek loses 0.40 rating points in its first three years, most of it in year one, and the newest raters still rate it lower than the number on the page. Hype doesn't decide how far a game falls; it decides how fast.

The decline isn't finished at three years

Ratings added recently still come in 0.23 below the displayed number at month 36. The running average carries the launch crowd's enthusiasm for years.

Hype changes the speed, not the size

The most-hyped games lose 0.19 points by month 6, quiet ones 0.10. By month 36 they end up in nearly the same place.

Trust a rating after about 13 months

Only 32% of games launch within ±0.25 of their 3-year rating. A simple adjusted launch rating gets 55% there on day one.
